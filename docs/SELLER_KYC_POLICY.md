# Seller Identification Policy

Written as an initial MVP response; legal validation is recommended later.

## MVP Principle

A seller must complete basic identification details and declarations before real activity. C-ton may perform checks, request documents, restrict activity or block activity after the fact or in exceptional cases.

There is no default manual admin approval requirement for every seller, no default human review queue before a first deal, and no PEP or sanctions check as a blocking MVP step.

## Required Basic Details

- Seller full name or business name
- ID, company number or licensed dealer number according to seller type
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Display name shown to buyers
- Authorization-to-sell declaration
- Declaration that the activity and offered products or services are lawful

## Operational Statuses

- Active
- UnderReview
- Restricted
- Suspended
- Banned

## Enforcement Model

If the required details are completed, the seller can continue automatically. Admin may later restrict, suspend, request documents or block activity if there is suspected unlawful activity, misleading content, fraud, rights infringement, delivery risk or consumer risk.
