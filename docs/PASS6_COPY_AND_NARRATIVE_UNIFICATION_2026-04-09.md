# Pass 6: Copy And Narrative Unification

Date: 2026-04-09

## Goal

Unify the visible product language so the active Siton surfaces sound like one product:

- main site
- seller workspace
- create-deal flow
- seller deal management
- public deal / payment / confirmation / tracking
- internal affiliate/admin surfaces

## What Changed

- Rewrote the main-site hero, proof points, and CTAs in one seller-first, link-first voice.
- Replaced old English CTAs and section titles across seller surfaces with clear Hebrew product copy.
- Reframed buyer payment messaging so it consistently distinguishes authorization hold from real charge.
- Reframed affiliate and admin as internal surfaces with cleaner, non-raw product language.
- Removed old primary-surface wording such as marketplace/demo-style language from the active bundle.
- Updated frontend tests so the new seller-first narrative and internal-surface framing stay enforced.

## Outcome

The active product now presents a more consistent story:

- Siton is the main commercial gateway.
- Sellers open and publish personal deal pages.
- Buyers join through a direct link.
- Payment messaging clearly describes hold versus charge.
- Admin and affiliate remain available, but read as internal operational surfaces rather than co-equal product journeys.
